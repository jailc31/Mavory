## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in the romance of culinary wanderlust and high-editorial food journalism. Evoking the refined sensibility of independent print publications like *Kinfolk* and *Cereal*, paired with the authoritative prestige of the Michelin Guide, the aesthetic balances tactile warmth with pristine typographic discipline.

The experience is tailored for culinary travelers, home gourmands, and cultural enthusiasts who seek stories behind heritage ingredients, regional techniques, and artisan traditions rather than quick utility recipes. The emotional signature is immersive, serene, and culturally authentic.

The style unifies **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Nuances**:
- Generous, breathing negative space that treats mobile viewports like pristine magazine spreads.
- High-fidelity food and travel imagery framed with soft, organic contours.
- Delicate hairline ornamentation and subtle tonal framing, rejecting cold sterile interfaces in favor of soft parchment and aged ceramic textures.

## Colors

The palette draws from natural terroir, cellar-aged wines, vintage cookware, and unbleached stationery:

- **Primary (`#722F37` - Vintage Wine):** The signature anchor. Reserved for high-impact interactions, primary call-to-actions, active navigation states, and selected filters.
- **Secondary (`#34483C` - Deep Sage / Forest):** Grounding botanical accent. Denotes botanical ingredients, terroir indicators, regional origins, and curated cultural collections.
- **Tertiary (`#C49A54` - Antique Gold):** Used sparingly for Michelin-style ratings, saved favorites, fine metallic rules, award badges, and exclusive editorial bookmarks.
- **Neutral Primary (`#302724` - Dark Roast Brown):** A softened black providing readable contrast without the synthetic harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#6E615A` - Muted Warm Earth):** Applied to secondary metadata, author bylaws, ingredient measurements, and caption text.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F7EFE2` - Warm Cream):** The base background wash establishing tactile warmth.
- **Surface Elevated (`#FDFBF7` - Soft Warm Ivory):** Applied to recipe cards, modal sheets, and interactive containers.
- **Surface Sunken (`#EDE3D4` - Pressed Parchment):** Used for input fields, chip inactive fills, and recessed card sections.
- **Borders & Rules (`#E5DAC8` - Subtle Warm Tan):** Delicate dividers and hairline contours.

## Typography

Typography acts as the editorial spine:
- **Editorial Voice (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for the masthead, issue headings, recipe titles, and culinary narratives. Uses italic variants for poetic subtitles and cultural glossary terms.
- **Interface Structure (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean functional legibility for cooking steps, time/temperature metadata, ingredient quantities, and navigational UI.
- All uppercase labels and badges utilize deliberate letter-spacing (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to mimic book and magazine typesetting.
- Body copy line-height is intentionally open (`1.6x`) to facilitate comfortable culinary reading in low-light dining or kitchen environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive, column-based fluid grid optimized for mobile touch interaction while maintaining editorial pacing:

- **Mobile Viewports (<600px):** 4-column layout, 20px outer margin, 16px gutters. Features full-bleed or cushioned editorial photography heroes that break margins with intentional asymmetry.
- **Tablet Viewports (600px–1023px):** 8-column layout, 32px outer margin, 20px gutters. Organizes recipe components into split culinary viewports (e.g., ingredients pinned on the left, culinary journal narrative scrolling on the right).
- **Desktop/Large Displays (>=1024px):** 12-column layout centered with a maximum width of 1180px, flanked by margins that honor fine book margins.

Spacing Rhythm:
- Component internals rely on strict 4px base increments, using `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), and `space-md` (16px) for compact tags, timestamps, and card insets.
- Section-to-section narrative transitions use `space-xl` (36px) to 48px to evoke natural breathing room between course chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons clinical drop shadows in favor of warm ambient glow and tonal stratification:

- **Flat Stratification (Level 0):** Background Canvas (`#F7EFE2`) with low-contrast hairline borders (`#E5DAC8`, 1px solid) to structure horizontal article dividers and recipe step boundaries.
- **Ambient Floating Surfaces (Level 1 - Recipe & Discovery Cards):** Ivory containers (`#FDFBF7`) cast a warm, dispersed ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(48, 39, 36, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(48, 39, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Overlay & Sheets (Level 2 - Filter Drawer & Tasting Menus):** Warm ivory containers elevated above backdrop dimmers tinted in warm umber:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(48, 39, 36, 0.12)`.
- **App Bar & Bottom Navigation Bar:** Frosted ivory surface utilizing CSS blur (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed with a hairline gold/tan top divider (`rgba(229, 218, 200, 0.7)`).

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined, tactile contour language balancing classical structure with modern organic smoothness:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Standard corners use a 0.5rem (8px) to 1rem (16px) radius, conveying craftsmanship without cartoonish bubbliness.
- **Editorial Recipe & Story Cards:** Enclosed in `rounded-2xl` (16px) or `rounded-3xl` (24px) for hero discovery tiles, echoing rounded culinary ceramic plates and journal edges.
- **Metadata Badges & Micro-Pills:** Utilize continuous circular pill shapes (`rounded-full`) for cooking metrics, cuisine flags, difficulty tiers, and category tokens.
- **Image Treatments:** Featured recipe photographs have corner radii matching their parent cards, bordered by a subtle inner hairline shadow to prevent image bleed onto cream surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Vintage Wine (`#722F37`) with Ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle pill or 12px rounded contour, height of 48px or 54px for prominent CTAs. On tap, scales smoothly to `0.98` scale.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, 1.5px solid border in Vintage Wine (`#722F37`) or Deep Sage (`#34483C`), typography in matching color, hover/active fill tint at 6% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No background, Antique Gold (`#C49A54`) or Dark Roast (`#302724`) text with a delicate under-dot or hairline underline transition.

### Chips & Badges
- **Difficulty Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), delicate 1px border.
  - *Fácil:* Background `#34483C12`, Text `#34483C`, Border `#34483C30`.
  - *Médio:* Background `#C49A5415`, Text `#967130`, Border `#C49A5440`.
  - *Difícil:* Background `#722F3714`, Text `#722F37`, Border `#722F3735`.
- **Preparation Time:** Pill badge pairing a micro clock glyph with `label-md` uppercase text, encased in `#EDE3D4`.
- **Origin Tags:** Pill badge featuring miniature cultural flag emoji/icon + localized region name in `body-sm`.

### Input Fields
- Grounded in recessed ivory (`#EDE3D4` at 50% opacity or `#FDFBF7`), enclosed by a 1px tan border (`#E5DAC8`).
- Active focus state transitions border smoothly to Vintage Wine (`#722F37`) with a 2px diffuse glow ring in `#722F371A`.
- Placeholder text set in Muted Warm Earth (`#6E615A`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 20x20px rounded squares (4px radius) with tan border; checked state fills with Vintage Wine (`#722F37`) revealing a crisp antique gold checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular with 1.5px Wine border; selected state features an interior concentric pill dot in Antique Gold (`#C49A54`).

### Editorial Recipe Cards
- Ivory surface (`#FDFBF7`) elevated with the ambient brown shadow.
- High-ratio imagery (3:2 or 4:5 vertical) on top with subtle top rounded corners.
- Content zone highlights: Serif title (`headline-md`), origin tag, prep duration pill, and bookmark action icon pinned with gold touch feedback.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- Frosted ivory background (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.9)`) with backdrop blur.
- Active tab indicated by an elongated pill highlight in muted Wine wash (`#722F3714`), with icon and label rendered in Vintage Wine (`#722F37`) accompanied by an Antique Gold micro-dot underneath.
